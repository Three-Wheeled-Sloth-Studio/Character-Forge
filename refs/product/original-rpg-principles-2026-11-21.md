# Original RPG Guiding Principles

Date: 2026-11-21
Status: owner direction; mechanics remain hypotheses

1. **Intent-first play.** A player primarily communicates what their character wants to do. The rules engine and GM resolve it without requiring the player to name the underlying check.
2. **Classless identity.** Templates and archetypes offer optional quick-start packages, not permanent classes or fixed advancement trees. Character identity may change through play.
3. **Deferred and organic growth.** Choices can unlock from experience, discoveries, training and narrative milestones. Preserve player agency and causal provenance without requiring bookkeeping after every trivial action.
4. **Low friction, sophisticated internals.** Rules may be deep, but every added mechanic must earn its player and GM interaction cost. Quick resolution is the default; detailed rationale and full rules are available on demand.
5. **GM authority, rapid lookup.** Digital assistance can propose mechanics, risks, outcomes and citations at the point of need. The GM can approve, adjust or reject them.

## Product interaction acceptance hypotheses

- Player can express a fictional action without knowing a skill or numeric modifier.
- GM sees one recommended resolution, relevant circumstances and meaningful consequences first; explanation and source are progressively disclosed.
- An archetype can initialize competencies, but a character can freely grow outside it.
- A formerly unavailable choice can become eligible without recreating or invalidating the character.
- Advancement records distinguish event, available option, selected option, effect and provenance.
- The system never silently invents a rules equivalence when converting other systems' characters.

## Reserved for playtesting

Resolution curves (including proposed 2d10/2d12), degree of success, wound severity, resource costs, and advancement triggering/frequency are not yet fixed. Existing non-d20, lasting-wound, psychic/techno-magic, dark-horror and cyber/steampunk directions remain hypotheses in the bridge evidence ledger.

This is an original-RPG product contract, not a requirement to alter D&D, BRP or Fate native rules.
