# Warlock Level 1 guided slice

Status: implementation in progress on `dev`.

This slice completes the final SRD 5.2.1 class at Level 1 without treating Pact Magic as ordinary Spellcasting.

Key boundaries:
- Pact Magic remains D&D-native class spellcasting state with an explicit `pact-magic` mode.
- Level 1 retains one Level 1 Pact Magic slot, refreshed on a Short or Long Rest.
- Warlock retains two cantrips, two prepared Level 1 Warlock spells, Charisma casting, and Arcane Focus capability.
- One Level 1 Eldritch Invocation is retained as Warlock-native class state.
- Only invocations whose prerequisites are satisfied at Warlock 1 are offered.
- Pact of the Blade weapon and Pact of the Chain familiar form are use-time decisions and are not frozen at character creation.
- Pact of the Tome retains its current Book of Shadows selections: three cantrips and two Level 1 ritual spells from legal SRD lists that the character does not already have prepared.
- Direct creator selection must expose every legal option. Sticky acceptable pools constrain only explicit randomization.

After SRD Level 1 creator completeness, the next architecture step should be to implement a second RPG system deeply enough to challenge D&D-shaped assumptions, then derive the first serious cross-system character definition from evidence across both native models.
